Pick a scale degree, a Note Length, then click empty space on the timeline to place that chord. Set Quality Override above to something other than "Diatonic (auto)" to override every degree with that quality instead (sus4, 6th, 9th, etc.).
Grid
Drag the middle of a note to move it, or its left/right edge to resize it — both snap to the grid. Click a note to select it; shift+click (or double-tap on touch) to add more to the selection, then drag any of them to move the whole group, or use Copy / Cut / Merge / Split / Delete above. To delete a single note without selecting it: right-click (desktop) or press and hold (touch).
