Grid
Drag the middle of a note to move it, or its left/right edge to resize it — both snap to the grid. Tick "Select notes", click two or more touching notes to Merge, or one note to Split. To delete: right-click a note (desktop), press and hold it (tablet/touch), or select it and hit Delete.
